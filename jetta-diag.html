🔧 Jetta TDI Diagnostic Scanner

Tests every OBD2 protocol and PID — shows exactly what your Jetta supports

Step 1: Copy the command

Tap the blue button to copy the install command:

Set-ExecutionPolicy Bypass -Scope Process -Force; irm https://rotstrucking1-prog.github.io/Rotstrucking-app1/install_jetta_diag.ps1 | iex

Step 2: Open PowerShell on your laptop

Right-click Start button → "Windows PowerShell (Admin)" or "Terminal (Admin)"

Step 3: Paste & run

Right-click in PowerShell to paste, then hit Enter. The scanner will test everything and show results.

⚠️ Before running: Close the OBD2 Dashboard app first if it's open. Make sure the Vgate is plugged into the Jetta and the key is ON.
