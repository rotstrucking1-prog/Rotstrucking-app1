⚠️ Tuning Hardware Required The ELM327/Vgate adapter can read all sensors but cannot flash ECU tunes. To apply tune profiles, you'll need either:
• VCDS/VAG-COM cable (~$200) — for adaptation channels & basic adjustments
• Alientech KESS v2 clone (~$150) — for full ECU map flashing
• Custom Malone tune ($400-600) — mail-order ECU flash, most popular for TDI

Saved tune profiles will be ready to flash once you have the hardware. The app will auto-detect when a flasher is connected.
